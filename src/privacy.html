Privacy Policy

Farm Valley · last updated 2026-10-28

This game is free, has no accounts, and never asks for your name, e-mail address or any other personal detail.

Saved on your own device

Your progress (farm plot, cash, upgrades, equipment, workers, quests and play time) and your settings (sound and performance mode) are kept in your browser's local storage, under keys that start with fv_. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them; Settings → Reset progress deletes the progress.

The game also keeps a random device code (fv.device) and a code for the current play session (fv.session). They identify nothing about you; Reset progress keeps the device code so that a fresh start is not counted as a new player. Your saved progress also holds a random save code, so that progress carried to another device by the cloud save is counted as one player; Reset progress makes a new one.

Cloud save and videos on CrazyGames

When the game is played on CrazyGames, the same saved progress is also handed to the CrazyGames SDK so it can follow you between devices, and the rewarded videos you choose to watch are served by CrazyGames. Both are governed by CrazyGames' own privacy policy. On any other site neither happens.

Play statistics sent to us

To see how far players get and where the game is too hard or too slow, the game sends short play records to our own database (Google Firebase Realtime Database). A record contains:

  • the random device, session and save codes above;
  • your progress: stage, share of the plot harvested, quests finished, cash and play time;
  • every few minutes, how the play went since the last record: how many blocks you, your machines and your hired workers broke, how long you drove, ran out of fuel or had a full load, how long workers were blocked, idle or resting, your crew size, and how hard the rock you can reach is to break;
  • which buildings you enter and leave, when you open the equipment list, which machines and tools you look at in the machinery shop, and which free boosts and gifts you are offered and take;
  • what you buy in the game (upgrades, tools, machines, workers, research);
  • when you open and leave the game, how long it took to load, and its frame rate;
  • whether your device has a touch screen, the screen orientation, Perf. Mode, and your browser's language and time zone setting;
  • the game version, the website it is played on, whether the Google Analytics script could load, and the error message if the game fails.

These records contain no name, e-mail address, account, advertising identifier or location. They are used only to improve this game and are never sold or shared. To have yours deleted, write to the address below with the device code shown on this page in the game.

Google Analytics

The game also uses Google Analytics, a service of Google, to count visits and see which countries players come from and how long they stay. A few seconds after the game starts it loads a script from Google, which may keep a random identifier in a cookie or in local storage (names starting with _ga) and sends Google the page address, your browser and device type, an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address, and a few game events (the stage started or finished, the quests completed). Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. If your browser or an ad blocker blocks it, the game works exactly the same.
