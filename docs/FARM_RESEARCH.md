# Farm game research: what to learn from the genre

2026-10-28. For the farm version of our arcade-idle loop (harvest cells → items stack on the back → processing
house → market → cash). **Method:** web search of store pages, user reviews, tracker sites and industry articles.
No gameplay videos were watched in this pass, so the "feel" notes come from store copy, reviews and genre
conventions. Check the key ones by playing the games (skill `game-playtest`) before building.

## 1. Games studied

**Arcade idle (walk, harvest, stack, sell)**

1. **Farm Land: Farming Life Game** (Homa, iOS/Android, Mar 2021). The biggest farm arcade-idle game: Google Play
   100M+ (AppGoblin ~117M, 4.55 from ~726K ratings), iOS 4.8 from 95K, #4 by downloads among arcade-idle games in 2024.
   *Loop:* water fields, milk cows, shake trees, shear sheep, sell to town merchants. The cash unlocks map tiles that
   reveal new land, vendors, crops and animals; you can hire workers and build bigger barns. *Hooks:* new produce found by
   exploring, animals, land tiles. *Style:* bright low-poly, plain featureless people (a reviewer liked them). A
   subscription gives a bike, a parrot pet and +50 % barn space. *Complaints:* too many ads, grindy at high levels, no
   cloud save. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1563700162) ·
   [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.loltap.farmland) ·
   [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/arcade-idle)
2. **Harvest It!** (Homa, 2020, iOS 4.5 from 3.8K). One of the first arcade-idle games. *Loop:* swing a scythe through
   crops, bag them, sell, then upgrade the scythe, the bag and the farm, and crops get better farther out. *Feel/upgrade
   hooks:* the paid items are the design: **BIG Scythe** (a wider cut), x2 Damage (crops take several hits), Infinite
   Bag, EXTRA Farmer. *Complaints:* ads, the ad "x2" paying x1, helper farmers that "swing at nothing" and add no money.
   [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1537088062)
3. **Harvest.io: 3D Farming Arcade** (Azur Games, mobile + web, **on CrazyGames**, rated 8.7 with 7.6K likes; ~450K
   installs on one Android store alone). *Loop:* drag a tractor through crops; hay piles onto a **trailer that grows
   behind you**; circle rivals to steal their load. *Feel:* the load you carry is the score, visible at all times.
   *Style:* colourful smooth 3D. [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/game/harvest-io---3d-farming-arcade) ·
   [CafeBazaar](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.harvest.io?l=en)
4. **Craft Island** (Homa, 20M+ downloads). *Loop:* chop trees (they regrow "super fast"), carry wood, build houses,
   bridges and tool rooms on pads, open new parts of the island. *Complaints:* ads (some that "take 1 minute"), done in
   under 2 hours, wants more ways to spend wood and gold, a no-ads purchase that still showed bonus ads.
   [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/craft-island/id1552703591) · [MAF](https://myappfree.com/en/arcade-idle-genre/)
5. **Craftheim: 3D Idle Lumberjack** (AI Games FZ, iOS 4.6 from 27K). *Loop:* chop trees and rocks, sell at a
   market, upgrade the house, hire lumberjack minions who work while you are away. New islands open by raft or bridge,
   and axes and costumes are collectables. *Complaints:* pop-up ads in the middle of chopping, stuck at house level 6-7,
   repetitive. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1572431447)
6. **Gold Rush 3D!** (Rollic, iOS 4.6 from 16K). The closest "dig gold" arcade game. *Loop:* cut grass, drill stones,
   clean what you find, mix chemicals to separate the ores (silver, emerald, gold, amethyst), sell, upgrade; later
   updates added gold smelting and jewel crafting. *Feel:* every processing step is its own short, satisfying action.
   *Complaints:* "a video ad every ~12 seconds", broken no-ads purchase, upgrades maxed too easily, repetitive.
   [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1512891619)
7. **Ground Digger!** (Homa, iOS 4.4 from 7.1K). *Loop:* drill down through sand, ice and lava layers. Gems,
   treasure and **hidden keys for buried chests** come up; sell, then upgrade drill, tank and wheels; mini-bots dig
   alongside. *Complaints:* ads after purchase, crashes, lost progress. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1597728243)

**Idle / tycoon (theme and meta reference)**

8. **Idle Lumber Empire / Lumber Inc.** (Game Veterans; >$38M revenue, #6 hybrid-casual 2023). Sawmill chain: tree
   planters replant the forest, lumberjacks are trained, machines turn logs into products. A reviewer gave the art 9/10
   for "so many details, animations, and even props" and praised sound timed to each animation.
   [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1552153525) ·
   [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/the-hybridcasual-gaming-market-of-2023)
9. **Gold and Goblins** (Redcell / AppQuantum, 2020; publisher says >$100M revenue). Goblins mine gold in shafts;
   merge goblins, 100+ mines, card upgrades, offline income, timed events (Treasure Hunt, a summonable Goblin King).
   Shows how far "dig deeper for better stuff" can carry a game.
   [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/fi/app/id1529762599) ·
   [AppQuantum](https://appquantum.com/news/appquantum-has-earned-$7,000,000-for-gold-and-goblins-in-3-months.html)
10. **Little Farm Story: Idle Tycoon** (SayGames, ~10-11M installs, iOS 4.5 from 40K). Arcade-style farm with a
    story: clear land, plant, open workshops, craft goods, fill **customer and trader orders**, build tractors and
    houses. Story areas: Countryside, Turtle Beach, Tea Farm, Mine, Sushi Farm. *Complaints:* forced ads that grow with
    progress, 30 min-2 h upgrade timers, energy-limited trips, tool passes that expire after 7 days, huge orders.
    [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/6733244789) ·
    [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.h8games.littlefarmstory)

**Big casual and design references**

11. **Hay Day** (Supercell, 2012, still a top farm game). Drag a sickle across ready plots to harvest them. Wheat and
    corn never die. Bakery and other machines; truck and boat orders; a roadside shop; pets; neighbourhoods and a weekly
    derby; seasonal events; decorations. *Style:* smooth, warm, painted cartoon with rounded shapes, the polish bar for
    farm games. *Complaints:* "after level 10 collecting becomes a chore", never enough storage, long build waits,
    expensive diamonds. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/hay-day/id506627515)
12. **Forager** (HopFrog, PC then console and mobile). Hit trees, rocks, gold and gems; items pop out; buy land to grow.
    The designer: "a +10 % mining skill is way less fun than **a new pickaxe that causes rocks to explode**";
    automation is the payoff for grinding; new features open slowly.
    [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/when-grinding-is-fun-i-forager-i-s-compelling-approach-to-idle-game-design)

Two side notes. **Farm Crusher** (Mango Gamez) is a spinning-blade wheat cutter whose reviews are mostly about a
level-20 wall after area 2 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6755424304)). **Sakuna: Of Rice and Ruin**
(Marvelous, 1M+ sold) proves rice farming sells as a theme, and players called its harvest step too fiddly
([Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2021/06/sakuna_of_rice_and_ruin_dev_hoped_itd_sell_30k_copies_-_it_just_passed_one_million)).
Genre facts ([Supersonic](https://supersonic.com/?p=82671), Udonis): arcade idle = joystick + stacking + unlocking +
upgrades. D0 play time is 20-30 min and D1 retention close to 50 %. Grass cutting and mining are named as the genre's
"satisfying loops". Cut visual noise so the eye finds the action, and let the camera show locked land so the next
goal is in view.

## 2. Ideas to adopt (ranked by impact for effort)

Each is mapped to what we already have (Candy Miner / Quarry Crush code).

1. **Rice is mown while walking, never a stop-and-swing.** One sickle sweep cuts an arc of cells in front of the farmer
   (start 3 cells, upgrades widen it; this is Harvest It's "BIG Scythe" as a shape, not a number). The stalks fall in a
   fan away from the farmer, stubble stays in the shallow water, and a **golden sheaf hops onto the back**. Reuse the
   multi-block sweep (Roller / Inferno ring) with no pause in movement. *Highest impact, low effort.*
2. **Combo pitch on fast cuts.** Each cut within ~0.4 s of the last raises the swish a semitone (cap ~8) and shows a
   small "x12" streak by the stack. That makes a rice field play like music and costs a few lines in `audio.ts`.
3. **One sound and hit weight per material.** Rice: soft swish and rustle, no hitstop. Wood: hollow *thock*, 40 ms
   hitstop, a little camera nudge. Rock: clink and sparks, 60 ms. Gold: bright *ting*. Gem: chime. Hitstop and shake
   scale with the cell's HP, so light things feel quick and heavy things feel strong.
4. **Trees shake, then fall.** On each hit: the tree sways, leaves burst, a notch of chips flies (reuse
   `Shards.burst`). The last hit tips it **away from the player** (0.5 s fall, bounce, dust ring, small shake), it
   splits into 2-4 logs that hop onto the back, and a stump stays and regrows. Medium effort, the signature moment of
   the middle zone.
5. **Different motions for different tools** (the user asked for this). Sickle: low, flat sweep with a hip twist, played
   on the move (upper-body layer only). Axe: diagonal overhead chop that stops the feet. Pickaxe: straight overhead
   strike. The tool in hand changes on its own to suit the nearest target, with a quick pop. Fits the procedural
   posing in `characters.ts`.
6. **Old big trees and rich rocks as "boss cells".** They take many hits and show cracks or bark falling in 3 stages, a
   ring HP bar appears after the first hit, and they pay in a burst (many logs, or gold nuggets plus a chance of a gem).
   Telemetry says players stall in the middle game, and these give it a goal you can see.
7. **Customers with wishes at the market.** Villagers (and later trucks) queue at the market pad. Each has a bubble
   such as "2 rice sacks + 1 plank" and pays a tip for a full order. Harvest-for-orders hooks Hay Day and Little Farm
   Story players, here in arcade time (seconds, not hours). It plugs into `SalesStand`; keep orders small (reviews hate
   huge ones).
8. **Treasure in the rocks.** A rare cell holds a chest or geode (Ground Digger's keys and chests): a little fanfare and
   coins, gems or a free boost. Low effort, good surprise in the gold-mine zone.
9. **Fields that grow back near base.** The first rice rows regrow green → gold in waves (Hay Day: crops never die), so
   a new player always has an easy money loop by the mill. Only the first harvest of a cell counts toward stage clear.
10. **Processing houses as a show.** The rice mill has a water wheel that turns faster while it is fed, and sacks drop
    onto the tray. The sawmill blade whines and planks stack. The gold works pours a glowing bar, and the gem cutter
    puts a sparkle on each stone (Gold Rush 3D turned every step into its own beat). We keep one pad in and one tray out.
11. **The load is the score** (Harvest.io). Keep the single tall back column and give each item a clear shape: a tied
    sheaf, a log with ring ends, a rough lump, a faceted gem. Machines tow a trailer whose pile grows.
12. **Upgrades that change how you play** (Forager). Besides +power: an axe that fells young trees in one blow, a
    pickaxe whose strike cracks the next rock, a harvester that eats a whole row. Give one of these at each tool tier.
13. **Opening new land you can see.** The camera shows the next locked area: a bamboo thicket to clear, a broken rope
    bridge up to the mine hills, terraces behind a gate. Bridges and pads exist already; this is mostly re-skinning.
14. **An animal helper.** A water buffalo pulling a cart (a hauler worker) or a farm dog that brings loose items in
    (the magnet boost, made into a character). Medium effort, high charm, and very much our setting.
15. **Daily calendar from the harvest.** Daily gift = seeds, a boost can or a sack of gold, with a harvest-festival
    week (lanterns) as the first event. Low effort on top of what is planned.

## 3. Visual direction

**What the hits share:** saturated greens and golds, bright high-key light with soft shadows, big readable shapes,
plain ground with little texture noise, a camera at about 45-55° that shows the next unlock. Arcade-idle hits (Farm
Land, Craft Island, Harvest It) are low-poly with simple people. Hay Day sets the polish bar: smooth, rounded, warm.
Idle Lumber Empire was praised for small props and animations everywhere.

**Ours: smooth bright cartoon, rice-paddy countryside**
- *Palette:* ripe-paddy gold, young-rice green, sky-teal water in the paddies, warm red-brown earth dikes, yellow-green
  bamboo, cool grey-violet rock hills with gold veins, red and terracotta roofs, lantern red for accents. Late-afternoon
  warm key light plus a cool sky fill. Water reflections and glints make it look different at a glance.
- *Shading:* smooth normals, soft gradient ramp, light rim light. Bake vertex colours as we already do; no flat facets.
- *Ground:* shallow water paddies cut by raised dikes; terraces stepping up toward the hills; a dry stone threshing
  yard by the base; the gold mine as a timber-framed entrance in a rocky hill with a cart track.
- *Trees by zone:* bamboo (fast, light) → fruit trees (mango, banana, coconut, each a new kind) → old banyan or
  ironwood giants (the boss cells).
- *Farmer:* wide conical straw hat (reads well from the top camera), short-sleeve shirt in one strong colour, trousers
  rolled to the knee, a towel round the neck, rubber boots or bare feet in the paddy. Workers vary hat, scarf and
  colour, as `CREW` already does. Keep it a friendly generic countryside farmer, not a caricature of any nation.
- *Buildings:* thatched or tiled roofs, a stilt barn, the mill with its water wheel, a market with awnings and
  lanterns, a smelter hut with a chimney by the hills.

**Different from Building Dream** (flat-shaded low-poly, wheat and flower fields, fences, scarecrow, villager
market): no wheat, no fences, no scarecrow, no flat shading. Our signatures are water paddies, terraces, bamboo, the
buffalo, lanterns, smooth shading and warm sunset light. The UI should take its colours from the paddy (gold and
teal), not from Building Dream's.

## 4. Pitfalls players complain about

- **Ads are the top complaint in almost every game above:** "an ad every ~12 s" (Gold Rush 3D), ads mid-chop
  (Craftheim), ads that grow with progress (Little Farm Story), and no-ads purchases that didn't remove them (Craft
  Island, Gold Rush 3D, Ground Digger). We already run with ads off; if they ever come back, rewarded only.
- **Hard walls:** Farm Crusher's level 20, Craftheim's house level 6-7. Every gate needs a path the player can see.
- **Too short or too easy:** Craft Island done in 2 hours, Gold Rush 3D maxed out fast. Pace tool tiers across stages.
- **Waiting and storage:** Hay Day's storage and "chores after level 10", Little Farm Story's 30 min-2 h timers,
  energy limits and huge orders. Keep everything in seconds; no energy system.
- **Helpers that look useless:** Harvest It's farmers "swing at nothing". Workers must visibly cut, carry and earn.
- **Crashes and lost progress** (Ground Digger), plus requests for cloud save (Farm Land). Our CrazyGames cloud save
  covers this; keep it.
- **Sameness:** Craftheim and Gold Rush 3D reviewers call them repetitive. New zones need new verbs, not just new
  colours.

## 5. What not to copy, and a working title

**Signatures to leave alone:** Hay Day's barn and silo, its newspaper roadside shop, the derby and neighbourhoods.
Harvest.io's io rivals and load stealing. Gold and Goblins' goblins and merging. Craft Island and Craftheim's
islands, rafts and portal fights. Farm Land's subscription pet and bike. Sakuna's rice-goddess story and its stage by
stage rice growing. Every mechanic in section 2 is a genre convention; our own versions come from our setting (paddy,
mill, buffalo). Also: this repo still carries Bulldozer Master's decoded economy (`src/data/orig`, `ORIGIN.json`).
The farm game needs its own numbers and data before it ships.

**Names already taken or crowded:** anything with **Harvest** (Harvest It!, Harvest.io, Harvest Loop 3D, Harvest
Haven, Harvest Land Tycoon, Harvest Horizons, Farm Empire: Idle Harvest Inc, Idle Farm: Harvest Empire, Lumber
Chopper: Harvest Empire), **Farm Land**, FarmTale, Little Farm Story, Farmer Rush, Crop to Craft, My Dear Farm, Handy
Farm, **Fields of Gold**, **Gold Rush 3D!**, Grab Gold Rush, Gold and Goblins, Craft Island, Craftheim, Wood Inc.,
Lumber Inc., Isle Pioneer. "Paddy" is a rice-terrace board game, and Rice Dice and Tiny Terraces exist too. Note that
"Paddy" alone is also slang for an Irish person, so it should always sit next to rice or field words.

**Five title ideas** (web searches found no game with these names; check Google Play, the App Store, CrazyGames and
a trademark search before choosing):
1. **Golden Paddy**
2. **Sickle & Stone**
3. **Grain & Gold** (close to "Fields of Gold", but not the same)
4. **Rice & Riches**
5. **Terrace Tycoon** ("Tiny Terraces" exists, a different game)
